The Visionary Behind

Dr. Swathi
BAMS, MD (Ayu) Kayachikitsa

At the heart of Krishna Ayurveda Clinic is Dr. Swathi, a dedicated Ayurvedic physician committed to helping individuals achieve better health through the timeless wisdom of Ayurveda.

With a strong academic foundation and specialized training in Kayachikitsa (Internal Medicine), Dr. Swathi combines traditional Ayurvedic principles with a patient-centered approach to address the root cause of illnesses rather than merely treating symptoms.

Her practice focuses on promoting natural healing, restoring balance, and enhancing overall well-being. She is also trained in specialized Ayurvedic therapies such as Viddhakarma and Agnikarma, offering effective solutions for pain-related conditions.
